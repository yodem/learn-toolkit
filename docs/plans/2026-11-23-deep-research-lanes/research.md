1. NotebookLM Deep Research
- Duration: Google says "in a few minutes" and "may take a couple minutes to load" (https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/notebooklm-deep-research-file-types/ and https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270). One third-party test (Oct 30, 2026, a single run) took about 4 minutes. It produced a report of about 4,000 words with 33 cited and 6 uncited sources, and the top 20 were pre-ticked for import (https://kastorflow.com/guides/notebooklm-research-notebook). Fast Research took about 20 seconds and returned about 10 sources (some duplicates).
- It runs in the background, and you can keep adding sources and using the notebook meanwhile (Google blog and help page above).
- Import: the help page says you can "import all results" or select and deselect. The results include the report plus all sources, cited and uncited, so the report is importable as a source. Anything not imported is discarded when you cancel. Imports can be partial if source or usage limits are hit. It is 18+ only.
- Source caps per notebook (https://universaldigitalassistant.com/guides/what-is-notebooklm/ and https://9to5google.com/2026/05/25/google-ai-plus-pro-ultra-gemini-features/): free 50, AI Plus 100, AI Pro 300, Ultra 500 (the $99.99 tier), Ultra 600 (the $199.99 tier). Each source is up to 500,000 words or 200 MB (Google help page).
- Deep Research quota in NotebookLM, per day (9to5google, May 2026): Plus 3, Pro 20, Ultra 75 or 200. Free-tier daily quota: not stated in these sources. The Workspace admin table (https://knowledge.workspace.google.com/admin/users/access/turn-notebooklm-on-or-off-for-users, updated 2026-05-11) lists "Standard access" as 10/month, then 3, 20, 30 and 200 per day for higher tiers. The 10/month figure is for Workspace standard access, so do not assume it applies to personal free accounts.
- Concurrency per notebook: not documented in anything I found. Treat it as unknown and assume one run at a time per notebook.
- Fast Research can run alongside Deep Research, since the UI offers both as a toggle in the Sources panel. I did not verify running multiple Deep Research jobs at once.

2. Gemini app Deep Research (gemini.google.com)
- Flow (https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111): Add Files, then Deep Research, enter a prompt, Submit. Gemini produces a research plan. Edit plan is optional, then Click "Start research". So it does NOT auto-start. A UI automation must click Start research. Whether the plan auto-starts after a timeout was not found in any source.
- Duration: "usually about 5-10 minutes", longer for complex reports (same Google page). You can leave the chat, and it notifies you when done. Click "Open" to view the report in the Canvas panel.
- Limits: Google publishes no per-day number. Its help page says only that there are limits on "daily research requests" and "number of research requests you can run at the same time". Gemini warns you when you are close to the limit (support.google.com/gemini/answer/15719111; https://ohrenbench.com/gemini-deep-research-checklist/ confirms no published per-day figure as of 2026-11-12). Overall usage is compute-based (https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16275805). It refreshes every 5 hours up to a weekly cap. The plan multipliers are free 1x, Plus 2x, Pro 4x, Ultra 5x or 20x of Pro. Deep Research is rated "High" usage cost. Free users may find it unavailable at peak demand. Check the live limit under Settings, then Usage Limits. Pro and Ultra can also use the Pro model, while free uses Thinking. Ultra reports can include visuals.
- Export: Canvas panel, then "Share & export", then Export to Docs, Copy Contents or Share Canvas (Google help page).
- Into NotebookLM: there is no one-click "add to NotebookLM" in the Google docs I found. The documented path is Export to Docs, then add that Google Doc to a notebook via Add sources, Google Docs or Drive. Docs imported from Drive auto-sync every few minutes (https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270). The Notebook help page also lists "Gemini Chats" as a source type, and Gemini can use a notebook as a research source. See https://yesiamrealhq.com/chatbot/guides/google-research-stack and https://pblfuturelabs.substack.com/p/how-to-turn-one-research-prompt-into for the Doc-to-notebook workflow.
- Fallback: Copy Contents, then notebooklm-mcp source_add(source_type=text) works. It avoids the Docs and Drive step, but loses formatting and links.

3. ToS and automation risk (flag)
- Google Terms of Service (new version effective July 30, 2026, as quoted at https://terms.law/ToS-Watchdog/ai-services/gemini/) prohibit automated access that violates machine-readable instructions such as robots.txt. They also prohibit reverse-engineering services. This does not explicitly ban all UI automation.
- Gemini Apps Help community thread, Aug 17, 2026 (https://support.google.com/gemini/thread/459962771/...): a user said Google One Support told them that using Power Automate Desktop to send prompts to Gemini and copy responses "is not permitted". A community member also said such UI automation violates the policies. The user disputed that any specific clause says so. This is anecdotal, but it shows Support's stance.
- Enforcement tiers per Gemini Apps User Guidelines (https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18481641): warnings, loss of features, loss of Gemini access, or loss of other Google products. The guidelines list "generating large volumes of content for data mining or scraping" as a violation.
- Gemini API and AI Studio are the sanctioned programmatic route, but Gemini API terms exclude consumer use (https://aistudio.google.com/docs/terms).
- Risk summary: low-volume, user-triggered, human-supervised runs on the user's own session are a gray area. Support has said no, so account-level penalties are possible. Sustained or batch automation raises the risk.

IMPLICATIONS FOR SKILL DESIGN
1. NotebookLM lane: research_start(mode=deep), then poll research_status at about 30-60 s intervals with a timeout of roughly 10 minutes. Then research_import, which should include the report. Then check the source count against the plan cap (50 free / 300 Pro) before importing, to avoid partial imports. Note the 18+ requirement and per-plan daily quotas, and handle a quota error with a graceful skip.
2. Do not fire parallel deep runs into one notebook. Serialize them, or use separate notebooks.
3. Gemini lane: opt-in only, never default. Gate it behind an explicit user flag, Chrome being logged in, and an acknowledgment of the ToS gray area. Run one report per invocation with a human-visible tab. Never loop or batch.
4. Gemini automation steps: Add Files, then Deep Research, enter the prompt, Submit, then click Start research (required, as there is no auto-start). Poll for the Open button for up to about 15 minutes (5-10 typical). Then Share & export, then Copy Contents. This is more robust than driving Export to Docs, which needs a Drive picker in NotebookLM. Feed the text via source_add(text). Optionally, Export to Docs and add the Drive doc via source_add(drive).
5. Handle the case where the plan does not appear or limits are hit. Gemini shows a quota message, so detect it and fall back to the NotebookLM lane.
6. Make the timeouts, selectors and button labels (Deep Research, Start research, Share & export, Copy Contents) configurable. The UI labels changed in 2026, and the product itself was renamed.
7. Always treat the imported report as unverified AI synthesis (the cited and uncited sources and dated sources are unchecked). Log the lane, duration, source count and plan tier for later calibration. These limits change often, so record the date alongside any limits the skill embeds.
